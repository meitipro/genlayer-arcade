---
title: Limitations
description: What Samepage does not do, said plainly.
---

Samepage checks that two accounts describe the same deal. It does not check that the deal is fair, legal or kept.

- **No escrow.** It holds a good-faith deposit, never the payment for the work. Paying for the work happens elsewhere.
- **No delivery checks.** Nothing here looks at the posts, the logo or the trade once the work is done. An agreed record is evidence a dispute service can use afterwards.
- **Everything is public once opened.** Both accounts are on chain in plain text after both open, so Samepage suits deals that can be public: promotions, gigs, collaborations. Not confidential ones.
- **The judge reads text, not intent.** It labels what the two accounts say. Two people who mean the same thing but write a term so vaguely that it reads differently will get DIFFER, by design.
- **Dates need dates.** "By Friday" against "Oct 16" is DIFFER unless the texts make clear it is the same day. Write dates.
- **The notes are a reading aid.** They come from the leader alone and are never compared or signed. Read the two accounts before you sign.
- **Two rounds, then it closes.** A page that still differs after round 2 closes NOT AGREED with both deposits returned; open a new page to try again.
- **Studio Next is a test network.** GEN here comes from the Studio faucet and has no value.

The [evaluation results](/docs/more/evaluation) publish the golden cases exactly as they came out, misses included.
