## FAQ

**Is it a contract?** It is a record both sides wrote and signed: both accounts, six statuses and two signatures, public and timestamped. It is not legal advice and holds no payment for the work.

**Who sees my account?** Nobody until both have sealed; the chain holds only a hash. Once both open, both accounts are public.

**What if they ghost?** A side that misses a window loses its deposit to the other, and anyone can settle it once the window closes.

**Can one side keep their round 1 text in round 2?** Yes. Either side may reseal the same text.

**Can the model decide the result?** No. The model labels six terms; the contract computes SAME PAGE, DIFFERENT or GAPS from the labels, and validators must agree on every label.

**What if the check never finishes?** After the check window closes, `no_show` closes the page NOT AGREED and returns both deposits.

**Which network?** GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997.
