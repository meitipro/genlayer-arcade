---
title: The four results
description: SAME PAGE, DIFFERENT, GAPS and NO SHOW, and what each does to the deposits.
---

The validators return six labels. The result is computed from them in the contract, never from the model's words, so SAME PAGE means the same thing on every page.

{/* include: contracts/samepage.py#def:verdict_of */}

| result | meaning | deposits | record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SAME PAGE** | every term matches or is absent from both, and amount, deliverable and deadline are stated | back when both sign | an agreed record with both texts |
| **DIFFERENT · N** | N terms are stated differently, or by only one side | stay for round 2 | the differing terms, side by side |
| **GAPS · N** | no differences, but N of amount, deliverable and deadline are missing from both | stay for round 2 | the missing terms named |
| **NO SHOW** | a side missed a window to join, seal, open or sign | the absent side's deposit goes to the other | recorded against the address |

Each page has at most two rounds. If round 2 is not SAME PAGE either, the page closes **NOT AGREED** and both deposits come back, because disagreeing honestly is not ghosting.

## Both orders

The contract asks the rubric twice inside one block, once with each account first. A term the two orders read differently is stored as DIFFER, and the page says which terms split. Position bias is invisible to consensus otherwise: every validator builds the prompt the same way and would lean the same way.
