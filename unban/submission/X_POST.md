Every ban deserves a second look.

Unban is an appeals court for your Discord: write the rules and a penalty ladder once, a banned member appeals with a small fee, a moderator answers with the quoted messages, and @GenLayer validators rule by the written rules alone.

Overturned: fee back plus compensation, and a bot lifts the ban.
Reduced: the ladder's lighter penalty.
Upheld: the ban stays, in public.
