---
title: The bot and what it can do
description: A read-only Discord bot that applies rulings with the server's own token.
---

The bot in `unban/bot` polls `list_appeals(court_id, "DONE", 0, 50)` every minute and, for each ruling it has not applied yet:

| Ruling | What the bot does in Discord |
| --- | --- |
| OVERTURNED, DEFAULTED | removes the ban (`DELETE /guilds/{guild}/bans/{user}`) |
| REDUCED | removes the ban, then applies the ladder level from its config, for example a timeout of 24 hours |
| UPHELD | nothing |

It finds the member by matching the handle at the start of the case line against the server's ban list.

<Callout type="warn" title="What it cannot do">
It holds no wallet key and signs nothing. It cannot change a ruling, file anything, or move money. Without the bot, the ruling
and the community's refusal to apply it are both public.
</Callout>

It remembers what it has applied in a local `state.json`, so a restart never applies a ruling twice.
