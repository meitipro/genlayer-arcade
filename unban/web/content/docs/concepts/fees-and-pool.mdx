---
title: Fees, compensation and the pool
description: Where every wei sits, and the one method that pays.
---

Every wei the contract holds sits in exactly one place, and `tests/test_direct.py` checks it after every call:

- a court's **pool**, free to reserve;
- a court's **reserved** amount, one compensation per open appeal that found the pool funded;
- the **fee** of every open appeal;
- a member's **balance**, owed after a ruling.

## Reserve on filing

`appeal` moves one compensation from the pool to reserved. A ruling for the member therefore never finds the pool empty. If the
pool holds less than one compensation, nothing is reserved and only the fee is at stake.

## Settlement

`judge` and `default` only move balances:

| Outcome | Fee | Reserved compensation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OVERTURNED, DEFAULTED | to the member | to the member |
| REDUCED | to the member | back to the pool |
| UPHELD | to the pool | back to the pool |

## Withdraw is the only payout

`withdraw()` pays the caller's balance as a top-level transfer. On Studio Next a value transfer is funded only at the root of a
transaction, so judging never pays anyone directly; the site and the scripts add the External fee allocation a payout needs.
The same call also returns to a court **owner** the free pool of every court they own that has no open appeal: that is how an
unused pool is reclaimed. Money already reserved for an open appeal is never part of it.

Anyone can add to a pool with `fund(court_id)`.
