---
title: The four rulings
description: UPHELD, REDUCED, OVERTURNED and DEFAULTED, what each means, and what moves.
---

| Ruling | Meaning | Money | The bot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OVERTURNED | The evidence does not show the cited rule, or any written rule, broken. | Fee back plus compensation from the pool | Lifts the ban |
| REDUCED · P*n* | A rule was broken, but the ladder sets a lighter penalty than the one applied. | Fee back | Replaces the ban with P*n* |
| UPHELD | The evidence shows the cited rule broken and the penalty fits the ladder. | Fee goes to the pool | The ban stays |
| DEFAULTED | No moderator answered within the response window. | Fee back plus compensation | Lifts the ban, never judged |

## Lifecycle of one appeal

```text
FILED --respond--> ANSWERED --judge--> UPHELD | REDUCED | OVERTURNED
  |
  +--no answer in time, default()--> DEFAULTED
```

`judge` opens once the member has replied, or once the 24 hour reply window has passed. `default` opens once the court's
response window has passed with no answer. Anyone can call either; who calls changes nothing.

## How a REDUCED level is checked

The normaliser in the contract refuses a REDUCED level that is not on the court's ladder, or that is not lighter than the level
the moderator applied. The validators compare the verdict and the level, so the bot only ever receives a real, agreed penalty.
