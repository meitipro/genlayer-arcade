---
title: File an appeal
description: What a member writes, what it costs, and every outcome before signing.
---

Open the court's page and choose **File an appeal**. You write three things:

| Field | Limit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handle and platform | the case line, 8 to 120 characters | so the moderator and the bot can find the ban |
| Date of the ban | `YYYY-MM-DD`, not in the future | the rules in force before that day are the ones you are judged by |
| Statement | 1 to 1,000 characters | your side, in your own words |

You send exactly the court's appeal fee. If the pool holds at least one compensation, one is **reserved for you** at that
moment, so a ruling in your favour is never unpaid. If it does not, the form says so and only the fee is at stake.

## Every outcome

| Ruling | You get | The bot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OVERTURNED | fee plus compensation | lifts the ban |
| REDUCED | the fee | replaces the ban with the ladder's penalty |
| UPHELD | nothing, the fee goes to the pool | the ban stays |
| DEFAULTED | fee plus compensation | lifts the ban |

## After filing

1. A listed moderator answers within the court's window with the rule, the level and the quotes.
2. You may **reply once**, within 24 hours, in up to 600 characters. This is where you dispute a quote.
3. Judging opens after your reply or when the 24 hours end. Anyone can call it, including you.
4. When a ruling pays you, **Withdraw** on the appeal page sends your balance to your wallet. It lands at finality.

<Callout title="Limits">
One open appeal per case, and one appeal per address per court every seven days.
</Callout>
