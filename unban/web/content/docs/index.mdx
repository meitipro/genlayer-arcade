---
title: Introduction
description: Unban is an appeals court a community sets up once, ruled by GenLayer validators from the community's own written rules.
---

Crypto communities ban members every day, during raids, scam waves and heated price talk, and most of those calls are made
by one moderator in seconds. When the call is wrong, the appeal goes back to the same person, or to a ticket nobody is obliged
to answer.

Unban gives a Discord, Telegram or X community an appeals court:

1. The community writes **numbered rules** and a **penalty ladder** once, lists its moderators and funds a small compensation pool.
2. A banned member **appeals** with a small fee, naming the case and the date of the ban.
3. A listed moderator **answers** with the rule broken, the penalty applied and the quoted messages. The member may reply once.
4. GenLayer validators **rule** from the written rules alone: the ban is upheld, reduced to the penalty the ladder sets, or overturned. An appeal nobody answers in time is defaulted to the member.
5. Balances settle, the member withdraws, and the community's **bot** reads the ruling and applies it.

<Callout type="warn" title="Appeals are public">
The case, the statement, the quotes and the ruling are stored on chain. Write rules so that evidence never needs personal data.
</Callout>

## What Unban is not

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Not a moderation bot | Moderators still ban and mute. Unban reviews the call when a member appeals. |
| Not fact-finding | The judge works from the quoted evidence. It cannot check what was really said in the chat. |
| Not a platform ban | It covers a community's own rules, not Discord or Telegram terms of service. |
| Not private | Appeals and evidence are public. |
