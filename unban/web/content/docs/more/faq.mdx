## Who decides?

GenLayer validators. Each one runs the judge prompt over the same rules, record and appeal, and the ruling stands only when they agree on the verdict and, for a reduced ban, the level. No moderator, owner or operator can change a ruling.

## What if the quote is fake?

The judge treats quotes as accurate unless the moderator's own record contradicts them. Every quote is public next to the moderator's address, and the member's reply is where a quote gets disputed.

## Can a court owner take the pool back?

Only the unreserved part, and only while no appeal is open. Compensation reserved for an open appeal stays until that appeal is settled.

## Which network?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. Reads need no wallet; every write is signed in your browser. See [Addresses and network](/docs/reference/addresses).

## Is there a model key on the server?

No. The site holds no private key and no model key; judging happens on the validators.
