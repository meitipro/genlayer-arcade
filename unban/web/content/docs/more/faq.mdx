## Who decides?

Several GenLayer validators each read the same rules and the same record and ask a model the same question. A ruling is stored
only when they agree on the verdict and, for REDUCED, on the level.

## What if the quote is fake?

The judge rules on the record as both sides left it. A quote is disputed in the member's reply, in public, next to the
moderator's address. A record that contradicts itself is overturned.

## Can I appeal twice?

One open appeal per case, and one appeal per address per court every seven days.

## Why does a refused transaction show as decided on the explorer?

Validators can agree that a refusal is the correct result. The site only calls a write successful when it was accepted and
the contract returned; otherwise it shows the contract's sentence. See [Errors](/docs/reference/errors).

## Which network?
