---
title: File an appeal
description: What a banned member does, from filing to withdrawing.
---

## Before you file

Open the court's page and read its rules and ladder. A ruling only ever asks whether **a written rule** was broken and whether the penalty is the one the ladder sets. The form shows, before you sign, what each of the four rulings would pay you, including when the pool holds less than one compensation or nothing at all.

## The appeal

| Field | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Case | 120 characters | Your handle and platform. Include your Discord user id so the bot can apply the ruling. |
| Date of the ban | today or earlier | Decides which version of the rules judges your ban |
| Statement | 1,000 characters | In your own words |
| Fee | exactly the court's fee | Sent with the call |

One open appeal per case, and one appeal per address per court every 7 days.

## After you file

1. A listed moderator answers with the rule cited, the level applied and the quoted messages, inside the court's response window. If nobody does, anyone can grant your appeal by default.
2. You may reply **once**, within 24 hours of the answer, in at most 600 characters. This is where you dispute a quote.
3. Judging opens after your reply or when the 24 hours end. Anyone can call it; the page shows "Validators are reviewing" while consensus runs.
4. Your balance shows on the appeal page. **Withdraw** sends it to your wallet in one top-level transfer.

<Callout>The judge treats the quotes as accurate unless the moderator's own record contradicts them, for example when the quoted messages come from a different handle than yours. Say so in your reply.</Callout>
